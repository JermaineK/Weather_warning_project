Two layers. Tropical-storm formation (10–35° latitude, next 0–5 days) and explosive mid-latitude lows (25–70°, next 3 days). The alerts come from boosted-tree models that were blind-tested before release. The beta's alerts are identical to the main app's.

New in the beta

  • Overview tab: the day at a glance, with clickable lists of alert regions and active storms.
  • Room to grow (experimental): each active storm's wind ÷ its potential intensity, the most the local sea and air can sustain. In a pre-registered test on unseen years (2015–24), storms far below that ceiling mostly strengthened the next day and storms near or above it mostly weakened. The GEFS version used here agrees with the ERA5 version that was tested (correlation 0.97) but is least reliable poleward of about 35°. It never changes the alerts.
  • Layer menu, light and dark themes, wind units, shareable links, keyboard shortcuts, and a data-freshness indicator.

Forecast tab: the physical formula re-evaluated with each forecast hour's ingredients, at fixed grid points. A disturbance that moves away makes the chance at its old position fall, so follow the colour across the map, not one point.

"Why here?" uses simpler physical formulas, fitted on 2010–16 and tested on unseen years. Each bar is that ingredient's push on the odds compared with a typical point, shown as a multiplier.

Shared structure of the vortices: a seed of spin, an energy source that has to switch on, and a feedback loop that locks the vortex in once it is strong.
