All days in this folder, one frame per day (made by make_gif.py after each run).

Two layers. Tropical-storm formation (10–35° latitude, next 0–5 days) and explosive mid-latitude lows (25–70°, next 3 days). The alerts come from boosted-tree models that were blind-tested before release.

"Why here?" uses simpler physical formulas, fitted on 2010–16 and tested on unseen years. Each bar is that ingredient's push on the odds compared with a typical point, shown as a multiplier.

Shared structure of the vortices: a seed of spin, an energy source that has to switch on, and a feedback loop that locks the vortex in once it is strong.

What differs: vertical wind shear brakes tropical storms, but fuels mid-latitude lows through the temperature contrast. Strong net spin switches the tropical shear brake off ("lock-in").
